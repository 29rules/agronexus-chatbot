AGRONEXUS TRADING — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Who is Agronexus Trading?
A: Agronexus Trading Co. is a Canadian-registered agro-commodity import/export company with operations in India. We source directly from farmers and FPOs (Farmer Producer Organizations) in Gujarat and Rajasthan, ensuring quality at origin. Our Canadian office handles distribution and packaging.

Q: Where are you based?
A: We operate between Toronto, Canada and Gujarat, India. Our sourcing office is in Gujarat and our Canadian operations handle import, packaging, and distribution in the GTA.

Q: Do you sell retail or wholesale only?
A: We primarily serve wholesale buyers: distributors, grocery chains, Indian food importers, and restaurants. Minimum orders apply. For retail inquiries, contact us directly.

Q: Can I get a sample before placing an order?
A: Yes. We offer 10 KG samples for $50 USD, which is credited toward your first order. Contact info@agronexustrading.in to request samples.

Q: How do I place an order?
A: Email us at info@agronexustrading.in with: product name, quantity needed, destination port, and preferred delivery timeframe. We'll send a proforma invoice within 24 hours.

Q: Are your products organic?
A: Organic variants are available for cumin, sesame, and fennel seeds. Please specify organic requirement when requesting a quote. Additional lead time may apply for certified organic lots.

Q: What makes Agronexus different from other exporters?
A: Three things: direct farmer relationships (no middlemen), our own mango farm in Gujarat for Kesar mangoes, and a Canadian presence that simplifies import logistics for Canadian buyers.
